## Format rules for this template

- 1 page maximum. If it does not fit, cut something.
- High severity material incidents only in section 2. Do not include Medium or below.
- No alert volume metrics anywhere on this page. No counts of blocked phishing emails, failed logins, or total alerts triaged.
- Stage ratings in section 1 use CISA ZTMM v2.0 language only: Traditional, Initial, Advanced, Optimal. Do not substitute numeric scores or custom rating labels.
- Every number on this page should tie to a decision the board can make or a trend that informs the next investment cycle.
- Update cadence: whenever the board requests a current posture view, or on a fixed pre-meeting schedule agreed with the board chair.
