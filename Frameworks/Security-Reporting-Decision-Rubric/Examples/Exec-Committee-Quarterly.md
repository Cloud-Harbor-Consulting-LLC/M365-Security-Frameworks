## Format rules for this template

- 1 page front and back maximum.
- High severity material incidents only in section 2. Medium severity appears in the CISO Monthly, not here.
- Every investment request in section 3 must name the risk it addresses and the consequence of deferring. Requests without a named risk will not get approved.
- Risk acceptances in section 4 require a named owner and an expiry date. Open-ended acceptances are not risk management, they are avoidance.
- No technical jargon in sections 1, 3, or 5. The executive committee reader should be able to read every row without a technical briefer present.
